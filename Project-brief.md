# My Project brief

## The question
How much has the built-up area of Lagelu and Ibadan North expanded since 2020?

## The data I need
- GRID3 settlement extents - https://data.grid3.org/datasets/ - GeoPackage - 1.9GB
- WorlPop - worldpop.org - https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=17208
- built-up surface layers - QuickOSM plugin, inside QGIS; portal.opentopography.org
- Satelite Imagery - earthexplorer.usgs.gov
- Statistics - nigerianstat.gov.ng
